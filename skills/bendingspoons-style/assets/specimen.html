We take on cinemas that have gone dark and fold them into one shared back office for booking, repairs, and payroll.

Then we hand each one back to a local crew. Five people, their own programme, their own keys.

We do not flip buildings. A house we reopen stays open.

The back office

Reelhouse

Print and file booking

Reelhouse books every title for every screen, tracks each print and drive in transit, and flags a clash before it reaches a poster.

Usher

Ticketing and seating

Usher sells seats online and at the door from one map, holds rows for members, and settles with distributors overnight.

Gaffer

Repairs and spares

Gaffer logs every fault in every booth, keeps a shared shelf of lamps and belts, and sends the nearest engineer.

Foyer

Membership

Foyer runs passes that work in any house, remembers what a member has seen, and writes the monthly letter.
